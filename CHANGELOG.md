# Changelog

Each release's changes, newest first. The versions follow `Cargo.toml`.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-16

The first release.

- A daily schedule with fades, which may cross midnight.
- A Temperature slider that runs down to pure red, where only red is left.
- A brightness cut on top of it.
- Red Now and Pause Schedule in the menu bar.
- Your displays' own colors come back when Arrakis quits, and the tint is put
  back after sleep and display changes.
- Its own Metal painter, with GL as the fallback.
- Settings in `settings.json`, which you can edit by hand.
